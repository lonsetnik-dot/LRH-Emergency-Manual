01Pulseless VF / VT

CALL A CODE · START CPR · ATTACH THE DEFIBRILLATOR
NAME
"Pulseless — starting CPR, calling a code." Say it out loud.
CLAIM
Compressor · airway · meds/IV-IO · defibrillator/recorder — assign aloud, rotate compressor every 2 min.
AIM
High-quality CPR with minimal interruption. Shock early and often. Find and fix a reversible cause.
0:00
CPR CYCLE — CHECK RHYTHM/PULSE AT 2:00
SHOCKS
0
DEFIBRILLATION
  • Biphasic: 120–200 J first shock (per your device's manufacturer label); equal or escalating for subsequent shocks.
  • Monophasic: 360 J for all shocks.
  • Resume compressions immediately after each shock — minimize the pause to <10 s.
EPINEPHRINE
  • 1 mg IV/IO (1:10,000, i.e. 0.1 mg/mL — 10 mL prefilled syringe), every 3–5 min, flush with 20 mL.
  • Typically first given after the 2nd shock, without interrupting compressions.
ANTIARRHYTHMIC
  • Amiodarone — 1st dose 300 mg IV/IO push after the 3rd shock; 2nd dose 150 mg after a later shock if still in VF/VT. Cumulative max 2.2 g/24 h.
  • Lidocaine (alternative) — 1st dose 1–1.5 mg/kg; 2nd dose 0.5–0.75 mg/kg. Max 3 mg/kg total.
HIGH-QUALITY CPR TARGETS
  • Rate 100–120/min · depth 5–6 cm (2–2.4 in) · full recoil · minimize interruptions.
  • Rotate the compressor every 2 min cycle to prevent fatigue-related decay in compression quality.
REVERSIBLE CAUSES — H's AND T'sCHECK EVERY CYCLE

Energy levels and exact interval are device- and protocol-specific — verify against your defibrillator's manufacturer label and LRH's own ACLS order set.
