Disposition logic (per posted protocol)
  • Rule-in zone (any hs-Trop ≥50 or Δ≥15) → consider cardiology consult if clinically appropriate.
  • D/C home (<5 & Δ<4 on the 0/1-hr pair; or, on a single 0-hr, ≤3, or <4 with pain>3h) → discharge, outpatient follow-up as needed. Once a 1-hr value exists, the 0/1-hr Δ governs — a low 0-hr with a rising 1-hr reflexes to 3 h rather than discharging.
  • Reflex to 3 h (< 50 & Δ 4–14) → repeat troponin; Δ≥15 rules in, else AMI ruled out.
  • AMI ruled out → apply HEART score:
    • 0–3 (low) → D/C, no stress test, F/U PCP
    • 4–6 (medium) → consider outpatient stress testing within 72 hours–1 week, using clinical acumen in conjunction with patient preference — emphasis on rapid follow-up with cardiology or PCP rather than a fixed hour count; document that follow-up is arranged, along with clear return precautions.
    • 7–10 (high) → consider stress testing prior to d/c, using clinical acumen in conjunction with patient preference → D/C w/ PCP F/U or cardiology consult

Outpatient stress-test timing after AMI is ruled out: ACC/AHA and ACEP guidance describes a 72-hour–1-week window for medium-risk patients discharged with shared decision-making — strict completion within 72 hours is the older, more commonly cited figure, but is often impractical and has not been consistently shown to improve outcomes in carefully selected patients; the emphasis in more recent guidance is on rapid follow-up (cardiology or PCP) and documented return precautions rather than a fixed hour count.
