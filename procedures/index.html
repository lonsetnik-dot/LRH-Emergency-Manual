02Resuscitative Thoracotomy

TAMPONADE ON ULTRASOUND · IN EXTREMIS
⚠ LOCALIZE THIS CARD: confirm whether ED thoracotomy is within your facility's scope, who is credentialed, and your surgical-backup/transfer reality before an event — a rural ED without immediate surgical backup must decide this in advance, not at the bedside.
PATIENT SELECTION — THE DECISION IS THE PROCEDURE
SEQUENCE — HIGH LEVEL
Sources: The Procedures Course, Thoracotomy Reference — The Alfred STAR Program (Alfred Health, Melbourne): reversible-pathology framing (tamponade from low-pressure chamber laceration, occult without external signs/ECG changes), ultrasound-driven selection, poor outcome with no signs of life + PEA at arrival (Alfred Trauma Registry 2008–2013: 9 survivors of 27 ED thoracotomies), reserve for in-extremis patients with proven tamponade. Clamshell/pericardiotomy sequence condensed from standard references (EMCrit/Alfred clamshell convention) where the STAR page was not fully captured — verify against the full STAR course.

04Emergency Burr Hole (Craniotomy)

HERNIATING + NEUROSURGERY FAR AWAY
⚠ LOCALIZE THIS CARD: a last-resort temporizing procedure for a herniating patient with a CT-proven extra-axial hematoma when neurosurgical care is hours away. Agree in advance with your referral neurosurgeons whether, when, and how this would ever be done here — ideally with them on the phone in the moment.
DECISION & SITE
Sources: The Procedures Course, Craniotomy Reference — The Alfred STAR Program (Alfred Health, Melbourne): burr-hole landmark figure (frontal hole 3 cm off midline near the coronal suture; temporal hole above the zygomatic arch over the middle meningeal artery; external landmarks — external auditory meatus, mastoid, sigmoid sinus) — the figure above is a redrawn schematic of that diagram. Decision framing condensed from standard emergency-neurosurgery references where the STAR page was not fully captured — verify against the full STAR course.

05Lateral Canthotomy & Cantholysis

ORBITAL COMPARTMENT SYNDROME · 60–90 MIN
DECISION & TECHNIQUE
Sources: The Procedures Course, Quick Reference (lateral canthotomy content) — The Alfred STAR Program (Alfred Health, Melbourne): loss-of-tension + visible orbital fat as the decompression endpoint, re-assessment, post-procedure care (eye left exposed, trimmed polystyrene cup for transport, antibiotic ointment, urgent ophthalmology), pitfalls (failure to expose/cut the inferior canthal tendon as the commonest pitfall; iatrogenic globe injury as the most serious complication; freely evertable lower lid as the completeness check; 60–90 min optic nerve/retina ischemia time).
