07 ANNOUNCE IT · START THE CLOCK

Shoulder dystocia

CART6 · VAGINAL DELIVERY
00:00
Start the clock as you announce it. Roughly 30 s per maneuver.
NOTHING LOGGED YET
NAME
“Shoulder dystocia.” Say the word out loud.
CLAIM
Physician at the perineum · 2 assistants on legs · 1 suprapubic · timekeeper.
AIM
Deliver the shoulder in maneuver order. No fundal pressure.
HANNOUNCE & START THE CLOCK0:00
PPRESSURE — SUPRAPUBIC0:00–0:30
Heel of the hand just above the pubic bone, pushing down and toward the baby’s face. Steady for 30 s, then rocking. Fundal pressure impacts the shoulder further and ruptures uteri.
LLEGS — McROBERTS0:30–1:00
Two assistants, one leg each: hyperflex the thighs onto her abdomen, knees toward the ears. Attempt gentle downward traction with the next contraction.
EENTER — CORKSCREW1:00–2:00
Rubin II: two fingers behind the anterior shoulder, push it toward the baby’s chest and rotate 30° out of the AP diameter into the oblique. Woods corkscrew: add fingers in front of the posterior shoulder and turn the same way.
RREMOVE THE POSTERIOR ARM2:00–3:00
Hand in along the posterior curve of the sacrum. Follow the arm to the elbow, flex it, grasp the wrist, sweep it across the chest and out. This buys about 2 cm — often the one that works.
RROLL TO HANDS AND KNEES3:00–4:00
Gaskin. Get her onto all fours and repeat the sequence — the posterior shoulder is now the one delivered first.
!STILL STUCK — LAST RESORT>4:00
THE BABY IS OUTNEXT
Hand the baby off and go straight to the birth sequence — stamp the time of birth, then ask the four questions out loud.
→ 02 · TIME OF BIRTH + THE 4-YES CHECK
All four yes: skin-to-skin and delayed clamping. Any one no: clamp now, warmer, neonatal resuscitation (card 03).
