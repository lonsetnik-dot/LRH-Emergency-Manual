01Brain Injury Guidelines (BIG)

mBIG · MINOR TBI RISK STRATIFICATION
For blunt head trauma with a positive head CT. Not a screening tool for who needs a CT in the first place — for pediatric imaging-decision screening (PECARN), see the imaging references cited on your department's pediatric trauma resources.
⚠ LOCALIZE THIS CARD: confirm your facility's actual neurosurgery-consultation or transfer process (in-house neurosurgery vs. transfer center), who is called for an mBIG-2/3 consult, and your local repeat-CT ordering pathway. The categories and imaging thresholds below are from the published mBIG literature; the "who to call" step is site-specific and must be filled in locally.

02Level 1 Trauma Activation

HIGHEST-TIER TEAM ACTIVATION CRITERIA
Who triggers a full trauma team response. Criteria adapted from the CDC/ACS-COT National Guideline for Field Triage — LRH is not an ACS-verified trauma center, so this is the evidence base for a locally-defined activation tier, not an ACS Level I center standard.
⚠ LOCALIZE THIS CARD: "Level I/II trauma center" (ACS verification of a hospital's resources) and "Level 1 activation" (this hospital's per-patient response tier) are different things — LRH is not an ACS-verified/designated trauma center. Confirm locally: who responds to a full activation (ED physician, general surgery, anesthesia, on-call roster), expected response time, and the transfer trigger/receiving trauma center.
