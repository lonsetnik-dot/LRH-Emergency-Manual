BEFORE YOU START — IS THIS RESUSCITABLE?
This aid is for a witnessed arrest in a trauma patient, or a pre-hospital arrest <10 min from trauma. Do not start if there are injuries incompatible with life, or no signs of life for more than 10 minutes.
FIRST — COULD THIS BE A MEDICAL ARREST?
If you suspect a medical cause that came before the trauma (AMI, arrhythmia, seizure, stroke) — or the monitor shows VF / pulseless VT — run the standard ALS algorithm and shock. TCA priorities are different.

TREAT THE REVERSIBLE CAUSES — NOT THE MONITOR
Hypovolemia — stop bleeding, give blood
Hypoxia — airway + ventilate
Tension pneumothorax — decompress both sides

ROSC — POST-RESUSCITATION CARE
Pulse returned at 0:00
· Complete the primary survey · keep transfusing to clear targets
· Early damage-control surgery — straight to the OR, or activate transfer to the trauma center
· Blood pressure: aim SBP 90 (MAP 50–60); if head injury, SBP 110 (MAP 80)
· Keep warm · treat coagulopathy · calcium in range · recheck the chest before ICC insertion
