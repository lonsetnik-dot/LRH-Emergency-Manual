    Equipment Readiness7 standards
    Where every kit, item and size lives — searchable — scored against seven national equipment standards (NPRP, ACS, ASA, NRP, AIM, MHAUS, ASRA). Record a location or a gap as you walk the carts, then export the findings.
    Simulations9 sims
    Pillow-patient drills that chain the manual's real workflows — birth into dystocia into a blue baby, STEMI into VF into the code-status conversation. 10–15 minutes, no mannequin.
    Neonatal Resuscitationlive engine
    NRP as a driven engine, not a card: one tap stamps the time of birth, the clock runs the 30-second cycles, the heart rate picks the next move, and the SpO₂ target shown is the one for the minute you are actually in.
    Postpartum Hemorrhagelive engine
    Clock from recognition, a running blood loss you tap up as you go, uterotonics in order with the contraindications greyed out for you, and escalation prompts that fire on the trend rather than a threshold.
    Shoulder Dystocialive engine
    The head-to-body interval on the clock where everyone can see it, HELPERR one rung at a time with 30-second prompts, and a record of every maneuver and its time that nobody has to reconstruct afterwards.
