System map — procedures & physical cards

Every procedure card, plus any Codes card that has a poster, a kit or a cabinet label, and the artifacts connected to each. Green is connected, red is a gap. A Codes card earns a row here only once something physical hangs off it — that is why bradycardia is absent and massive hematemesis is not. The first two columns are the menu icon and the card figure, shown together on purpose: they must depict the same idea. They do not have to be the same drawing — a 46×20 glyph cannot carry an anatomical diagram — but if the glyph says one thing and the figure says another, the card contradicts itself. That is what had happened to the resuscitation line, where the glyph still showed a hub with ports after the figure had been replaced with a two-catheter comparison.

ICON — the menu tile glyph. CARD FIGURE — the infographic on the card (cropped here). POSTER — printable one-pager. QR — that poster resolves to this card. WHERE IT LIVES — derived from the cart map that prints the labels, not asserted here. CART LABEL — a drawer or shelf label in labels. KIT — item list checked across card, poster and label by verify_kit_consistency.mjs.

All thirteen procedure icon/figure pairs now depict the same idea — five were rebuilt to get there (CVC, TVP, pelvic binder, JADA, resuscitation line). Canthotomy and the Blakemore / balloon-tamponade kit are the two fully connected rows end to end, and are the pattern the rest follow: figure → poster → QR → stated location → drawer label → item-level kit.
