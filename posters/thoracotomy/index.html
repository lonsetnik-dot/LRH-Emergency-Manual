Resuscitative Thoracotomy (Clamshell)

INDICATIONPenetrating torso injury with signs of life in the last ~15 min, or a witnessed blunt arrest under ~10 min with a surgeon. Tamponade is the pathology you are looking for — it hides with no external signs and a normal ECG.

DO NOT START IF ANY: no signs of life + PEA on arrival (Alfred 2008–13: 9 independent survivors of 27) · obvious non-survivable injury · mass-casualty incident · no surgical support for what you are about to open.
  1. Decide
    Gate it against the box above, out loud, before you touch a scalpel. The decision is the procedure.
  2. Organize
    Say it. Name a surgeon to call, one airway, one transfusionist. Blood in the room before the chest is open.
  3. X · A · B · C
    External bleeding first. iGel or ETT. BILATERAL finger thoracostomies. Large-bore access, blood not crystalloid.
  4. Incision
    Splash prep only. LEFT 5th ICS, sternum → the table, through muscle into pleura along its whole length.
  5. Rib spreader — open wide
    Finochietto in, crank handle DOWN toward the axilla — handle up blocks the clamshell. Sweep the lung aside.
  6. Open the pericardium
    Tent with forceps. Long VERTICAL cut anterior to the phrenic. Clot out, deliver the heart, look at every surface.
  7. Close the heart
    Finger first. Then Foley 16 Fr (5–10 mL, clamp the port) · staples · pledgeted 3-0. Under a coronary, never through.
  8. Cross over if the right bleeds
    Blood on the right is the reason — a hard view on the left is not. Mirror it, split the sternum, clamp BOTH mammaries.
  9. Aorta · hilum
    Compress the descending aorta against the spine. Bleeding lung → divide the pulmonary ligament, twist the hilum 180°.
  10. Signs of life?
    Yes → blood, TXA, calcium, OR or transfer; mammary bleeding will start. No → stop, note the time, debrief.
EQUIPMENT — TRAUMA CART · RIB SPREADER SHELF
  • Scalpel No. 10 / 20 · heavy Mayo scissors / trauma shears
  • Finochietto rib spreader · Gigli saw, Lebsche knife or shears
  • Artery forceps · 3-0 nylon/prolene · stapler · Foley · pledgets
  • Vascular clamps ×2 (aortic + hilar) · internal defib paddles
LOCALIZE: agree in advance whether ED thoracotomy is in this facility’s scope and what surgical backup exists — decide before the event, not at the bedside.
