PROCEDURES · ICON SYSTEM
One grammar, two tiers
RULE 1
Anatomy in ink, action in red. The body is neutral strokes; the one red element is what you do — the cut, the tube, the band. Never more than one red idea per figure.
RULE 2
Same base, honest difference. Related procedures share a base drawing and differ only by their key idea: CVC vs Cordis is thin-triple vs one-big-lumen on the same neck; chest tube vs pigtail is straight vs coiled on the same ribs.
RULE 3
Glyph = one landmark + one action. Cards and door labels get the abstraction. Posters and checklists get the detail tier: same drawing plus dashed landmarks and a caption — never a different drawing.
RULE 4
Construction. 48-unit grid, 2.2 stroke, round caps, nothing filled except action tips. Dashed = landmark or "the thing that follows." Must survive 16px.
